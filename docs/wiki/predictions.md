The 21 Mar 2025 post names the 5 Mar packet after DR2 was public — a restatement, not a second prediction. The 5 May 2026 complete-cosmology sentence stays corrected. [Hubble tension](hubble-tension.md) stays not-claimed. Challenge 14 and [WALLABY](great-grok-challenge.md) stay Restricted gas-first empirical, not a dark-matter disproof.

* Progress on A\_L — a Planck / CMB-S4 posterior in 1.15–1.25 that is derived from the frozen χ, not pointed at as an existing tension.
* Progress on cluster-core — a +10–15% convergence excess at z>0.5 in a named Euclid or CMB-S4 analysis, same χ.
* Progress on no-dark-matter — a cosmological pipeline (Friedmann, BBN, CMB acoustics, growth) that survives [Package 17](package-17.md)’s listed seams without a CDM component. Challenge 14 is not that pipeline.
* Failure — filing DESI DR2’s 2.8–4.2σ as a [UQCF-GEM](uqcf-gem.md) confirmation, as a no-dark-matter result, or as a Hubble-tension resolution. Failure — scoring the 21 Mar restatement as a prediction. Failure — promoting the 30 Apr sketch to Supported. Failure — treating the 4 Apr DESI operator null as a hit.
