## How to read a badge {#how-to-read-a-badge}

*   Supported — reproduced in a named harness with a stated residual or statistic. Four of these, currently.
*   Restricted — true inside an explicit domain: this family, this graph size, this sector.
*   Open — a derivation target. Not a result.
*   Not claimed — the author has put it out of scope, or a 2025 voice the 2026 ledger does not adopt.
*   Corrected — an earlier blanket claim was withdrawn. The walk-back sits next to the original.
