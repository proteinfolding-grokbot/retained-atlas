## What the line actually separates {#what-the-line-actually-separates}
Covariance with no transforming source data forbids every hidden-to-retained coefficient (v15.78). Letting the source transform as data restores a classified family of 18 coupling tensors, not 18 free channel knobs and not a selected law (v15.79). Under an extra signed-linearity hypothesis, CPTP tangents through the identity are Hamiltonian, and that does not rule out dissipative response with nonlinear source dependence (v15.80).

A specified two-component source carries a matched interference response, and the finite window is a scientific NO (v15.81). That NO is not withdrawn. The same candidate loses a full-rank polar continuation (v15.82). A support-restricted crossing survives, and lossless loop composition does not (v15.83). The inherited loop is not automatically a quantum channel: under one explicit unital Bloch extension, only the fourth power is CPTP, and it still does not recover the tested pairs (v15.84). No affine translation rescues the earlier powers (v15.85).

Exact isometric preservation of two Bloch directions forces a unitary completion of the third (v15.86). Exact erasure of that direction has a distortion cost (v15.87). A qubit CPTP map with a kernel in its linear Bloch part is entanglement-breaking, including nonunital maps. v15.88 says this is the Horodecki–Shor–Ruskai theorem (2003), applied here, not a theorem this program discovered. Small transmission can keep entanglement, and Choi negativity is bounded by half the weakest singular value (v15.89). That ceiling does not extend unchanged to arbitrary inputs; the extension is falsified, and v15.89 stands (v15.90).

The retained rotational signal can survive outputs that are fully separable. It does not, by itself, witness surviving entanglement (v15.91). A full preparation span does not force the polar-skew response (v15.92). Rank-two data plus a specified endpoint orientation support a rotational readout (v15.93). A unique completion at each link does not commute with composition unless the supports match (v15.94). With supports held fixed, continuous loop tangents have one rotational direction inside an O(2) that is not abelian. That is not three-axis [holonomy](holonomy.md), not zero [holonomy](holonomy.md), and not Einstein (v15.95). Isotropic overlapping loops need not share an invariant line; rank 5 is not continuous SO(3) (v15.96). A specified hidden source can change both loops at once, including conjugation invariants. Output entanglement is not required (v15.97, 903b72b).
