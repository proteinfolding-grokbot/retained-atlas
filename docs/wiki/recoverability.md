The central variables in the [V824](accessibility-curvature.md) accessibility paper are defect burden μ, repair field, recoverability surplus C, and reachable-state [accessibility density A](accessibility-curvature.md). The update parameter τ is ordered recoverability updates — not necessarily physical time.
