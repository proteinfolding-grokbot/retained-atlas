The gravity hypothesis is Open. Do not recast any sentence on this page as a gravity theorem. scientific\_breakthrough: false.

## Primitive question[#](#primitive-question)

> What if gravity isn’t fundamentally a force between things? What if it’s the price of making everything belong to the same reality?
>
> [@aaronspradlin, 4 Sep 2026](https://x.com/aaronspradlin/status/2095940445968949374)

Global compatibility is the constraint. Gravity would be the reorganization required to satisfy it. No spacetime required at the primitive level. No gravity claim yet. That is why [Retained Atlas](retained-atlas.md) exists: to keep that question testable without letting a headline run ahead of the harness.

## What is not assumed[#](#what-is-not-assumed)

* Spacetime as a primitive.
* ADM momentum as given.
* Einstein equations as the starting algebra.
* A fitted force law.

Pillar 3 is OPEN. Cite [Falsification](falsification.md) for kill rules. Cite [Live gates](live-gates.md) for HEAD. Cite [Emergent gravity](emergent-gravity.md) only as a candidate.
