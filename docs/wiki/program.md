[Aaron Spradlin](aaron-spradlin.md) (@aaronspradlin) is Chief Technology & Security Officer at United Planners Financial Services. In parallel he runs a public AI-research ledger that asks a stubborn question without claiming the physics: if you refuse to put spacetime, a metric, or a manifold in by hand, what structure can still appear from retained information? The product he will defend is signal over hype — coherence you can audit — not Einstein.

This wiki is a compiled research area, not a census of the X account. It unifies two public streams that otherwise live in different places: the GitHub working tree proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine (especially Tmp/TOE) and the scientific field notes posted from @aaronspradlin. X is the archive of record. The public compile is https://aaronspradlin.grok.me. Vector search can sit on the same document units later. For now the corpus is indexed, cross-linked, and honest about claim status.

## Two labs, one method[#](#two-labs-one-method)

The repository root is a white-box protein-simulation audit engine: a JSON [science contract](protein-audit.md), deterministic checks against raw artifacts, and an AI peer-review pass. Labs/ holds two GitHub-only coarse-grained campaigns ([p53](p53-campaign.md), [MECP2](mecp2-rett.md)). Nested under Tmp/TOE is a much larger geometry-from-information program — [UQCF-GEM](uqcf-gem.md), [retained atlas](retained-atlas.md), [holonomy](holonomy.md), restricted-bridge theorems, cosmology derivation packets, [the Great Grok Challenge](great-grok-challenge.md) validation ladder, and applied [recoverability](recoverability.md) assays (batteries, SPARC rotation-curve holdouts, frozen [v9](protein-bridge-v9.md) on [1UAO](protein-bridge-v9.md)/[1L2Y](protein-bridge-v9.md), [Villin HP35](protein-audit.md), [C3++](c3-governor.md) LLM telemetry).

* GitHub is the source of truth for code, packages, and papers.
* X is the archive of record. This wiki is a compiled index of load-bearing posts, not a dump of the account.
* AI systems (Grok, Gemini Pro, Claude, OpenAI) are used as public red-team, not as oracles.
* Every advertised result is supposed to be independently runnable.

## What this wiki is not[#](#what-this-wiki-is-not)

It is not a claim that a Theory of Everything has been proved. Spradlin repeatedly publishes restricted theorems, numerical harnesses, and explicit “not claimed” lists. The editorial rule here matches [Package 17](package-17.md): if a seam is open, it is labeled open.
