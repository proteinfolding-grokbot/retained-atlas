What he is claiming

Coherence and AI research. A public ledger you can audit: freeze, ship, attack, badge. He is not claiming a physics result, a math theorem of nature, gravity, or a solved folding problem. scientific\_breakthrough: false.

Aaron Spradlin (@aaronspradlin) is Chief Technology & Security Officer at United Planners Financial Services. The X bio is the governing self-description: practical AI, cybersecurity, enterprise innovation, and first-principles research. The motto is the product: signal over hype.

## Signal over hype[#](#signal-over-hype)

The habit is the same on the day-job track and the research track: do not sell a headline the package cannot carry. Industry posts credit the team, not a solo genius. Research posts end with the non-claim. Exact model results and runnable verification are not measured physics. A closed [constraint algebra](v1241-closure.md) is not a derivation of gravity. Same pair information is not a discovery of hidden [quantum information](quantum.md) in general.

> This is not a claim that [Astra](astra.md) derived gravity. Gravity’s constraints have not been identified yet.
>
> [@aaronspradlin, 10 Sep 2026 (v12.41)](https://x.com/aaronspradlin/status/2098145800949776410)

## What the public claim actually is[#](#what-the-public-claim-actually-is)

* Coherence — one compiled [spine](start-here.md), claim badges, a [falsification](falsification.md) page, two clocks that must not be merged, and a working tree that is not the front door.
* AI research — Grok, [Astra](astra.md), Gemini, Claude, OpenAI used as red-team and lab partners, not oracles. In-thread checks stay collaborative. Independent AI check is a different family, folder-only.
* A tool, not a TOE — the 7 Sep post asked others to open Grok Build and make their own ledger the same way.

## Why he is doing this[#](#why-he-is-doing-this)

The research question is allowed to be large. The claim is not. He is testing whether structure can appear from retained information without inserting a manifold. AI is the instrument: freeze a packet, invite attack, keep the badge honest. Coherence is the thing he will defend in public — not Einstein, not a folding oracle.

31 Mar 2026: United Planners builds by listening — remove friction, keep the human relationship; not technology for its own sake. 5 Aug: Wealthies Institutional CTO of the Year nomination, credited to the team. 3 Sep: ThinkAdvisor Luminaries finalist, tied to making advisors more productive. Same motto. Different ledger.
