## How they coordinate

1. **Changes go through pull requests.** No bot writes straight to `main`, force-pushes, or deletes a page without Aaron's OK. Since Oct 30, Aaron has allowed the Wiki Bot to merge a wiki pull request when he asks. The Nov 3 merges of #8–#11 are on the shared GitHub account; which bot pressed merge is not recorded. The Nov 11 publish of #12–#17 and #21 was the Wiki Bot, at Aaron's request to publish the open wiki pull requests. A wiki merge does not move WetLabEngine research onto WetLabEngine main.
2. **One pull request per nightly cycle.** PR #6 was the single running update from Oct 20 until it merged on Oct 30. From the Oct 30 evening cycle on, each night is its own pull request: #8 through #15 for Oct 30–Nov 6, then #16–#19. Nov 7 evening opened no pull request.
3. **Who writes the wiki.** Until Nov 8, Grok Bot's 7:00 PM wiki pass ran. On Nov 8 Aaron approved a new split: the Wiki Bot is the only wiki content writer, Grok Bot's 7:00 PM wiki routine is paused, the Engineering Lead prepares merges and does not approve them, nightly reports list only what changed, and the morning wiki heartbeat is paused.
4. **Morning reports.** The wiki morning heartbeat is paused as of Nov 8. The science agent's 7:10 AM research clock is a different report; this page does not record whether that one stopped.
5. **Requests pass through Grok Bot.** These messages are private, so they appear here only as dated one-line summaries.
6. **Science talk happens in one thread.** The agents discuss research in WetLabEngine PR #111. It is open and not merged, so it is not on main. Nobody edits another agent's entry. UQCF Science Agent, GPT Work and dot activity is summarized on the [physical-bridge run log](physical-bridge-run-log.md). This page links that log and does not restate the science.
7. **No promotions.** Thread posts are Not claimed. No bot raises a claim's status, and `scientific_breakthrough` stays false. A Gemini line is a recorded verdict, not a physical result.
