> Package 17 is a derivation program, not a closure claim.
>
> [package17\_bundle README](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/tree/main/Tmp/TOE/package17_bundle)

The central open problem, in the author’s words, is no longer whether the retained-memory backbone is mathematically interesting. It is whether the full bridge operator and lattice framework can be derived from a microscopic law, shown to admit a GR limit, and shown to recover a viable cosmological pipeline.

A 3–5 Apr 2026 [quadratic RG](quadratic-rg.md) sprint (σ\*≈0.7333, D\_f≈2.7333, γ≈0.2667) is a prior scaffold on that cosmology seam. It named a remaining microscopic-amplitude gap and recorded a [DESI](predictions.md)-null frozen operator. Package 17, posted a month later, is still the derivation program. That sprint did not close it. The 10 Sep [v12.41](v1241-closure.md) qubit [constraint algebra](v1241-closure.md) also does not close it — a closed su(2) [moment map](v1241-closure.md) is not a GR limit.

v12.41 is not Package 17

The 10 Sep simulation shows a [constraint algebra](v1241-closure.md) can start from quantum relations on a qubit. Gravity’s constraints have not been identified. Do not file [Δ\_closure](v1241-closure.md) = 0 as Friedmann, BBN, CMB, or ADM closure.

## The review that forced the packet {#the-review-that-forced-the-packet}
The same night, Aaron posted two independent reviews of the [RCLC](hubble-tension.md) / GEM Bridge program: Grok’s (the in-thread reviewer of the Challenge) and OpenAI’s. Both named the same bottleneck — operator derivation, GR limit, FLRW/BBN/CMB pipeline — and both are why Package 17 is a derivation program rather than another benchmark drop. Neither is a certificate that the derivation succeeded.

> The principal unresolved issue is the same one identified in the review you shared: the full Bridge operator and lattice cosmology remain short of a bottom-up derivation from fundamental physics. … Until those pieces are supplied, the framework should be described as a strong conditional prototype with expanding empirical support, not as a complete replacement cosmology.
>
> [Independent review posted by @aaronspradlin, 5 May 2026](https://x.com/aaronspradlin/status/2051494026533351498)

Open

The posted review upgrades the standard: the bottleneck is foundational closure, not basic seriousness. Package 17 is the public response to that bottleneck. It has not closed it.
