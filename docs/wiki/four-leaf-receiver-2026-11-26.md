## What B can infer {#source-identification}

A *chart* is a local record of which leaves it holds. Several charts can hold the same leaf. Initially A holds leaves 1, 2 and 3. X and Y each hold only leaf 1, Z holds only leaf 2, C holds leaves 2 and 4, and B holds leaves 3 and 4. A is the only three-leaf chart. B is the only chart holding both leaves with exactly two initial holders. These descriptions still identify A and B if all chart and leaf names change.

Start B's record at that preparation. Allow zero or one legal withdrawal anywhere, then run the original v16.53 four-leaf normalization. A legal withdrawal leaves every leaf represented somewhere. B can read the following result on its first loss, without knowing who acted or when normalization began.

| Leaves remaining at B | Local Pin grade | What can be inferred |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **2** | 5 | **A withdrew leaf 1**, the only one of ten legal withdrawals that gives this reading. |
| 3 | 3 | One of seven other withdrawals outside B, or no initial withdrawal. |
| 1 | 7 | B itself made one of its two possible withdrawals. |

The Pin has grade 1 and each leaf grade 2. The local P1 rule assigns the Pin the grade $9-2d$ when B holds $d$ leaves. The count alone suffices. **The one-withdrawal bound matters.** If A withdraws leaf 2 and then leaf 3, B can also read two leaves without A withdrawing leaf 1. [Source census and two-cut counterexample](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/blob/de5056da7a5071a9634a3d5018fd3ec7e9fdc8ca/ResearchHistory/UQCF-GEM/research/physical-bridge/geometry_recovery_paths/receiver_event_order/four_leaf_local_passage/SOURCE.md) · [Eleven input cases, including no withdrawal](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/blob/de5056da7a5071a9634a3d5018fd3ec7e9fdc8ca/ResearchHistory/UQCF-GEM/research/physical-bridge/geometry_recovery_paths/receiver_event_order/four_leaf_local_passage/source.json).

## Why the first loss is guaranteed {#first-loss-proof}

Write $q$ for the fewest charts that together hold all four leaves. Initially the only minimum covers are A with B and A with C. To destroy both by one withdrawal, the removed incidence must be at A, on a leaf neither B nor C holds. Only leaf 1 qualifies. After that withdrawal $q=3$. Withdrawing leaf 2 at A leaves $q=2$.

The historical R caller computes $q$ from its incoming holdings. At $q=2$, its expansion first gives every chart all four leaves. A return to cover size two requires every chart to lose at least one, so its first deletion leaves three. At $q=3$, the routine first leaves each chart missing exactly one leaf. It keeps at least one leaf missing per chart throughout. Returning to cover size three requires every chart to hold at most two leaves, because a chart holding three could cover everything with one more chart. Its first deletion therefore takes it from three to two. All remote charts experience the distinguishing edit under independently chosen priorities in the two histories. For two cuts that leave the same $q$, the proof gives a common ordering in which all remote addressed edit histories coincide. [Full proof and equal-cover controls](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/blob/de5056da7a5071a9634a3d5018fd3ec7e9fdc8ca/ResearchHistory/UQCF-GEM/research/physical-bridge/geometry_recovery_paths/receiver_event_order/four_leaf_local_passage/RESULT.md).

B holds neither of the two source leaves compared above. Its complete row of pairwise work measurements immediately after the cut is identical across those two arms. Across all **17,280** paired chart and leaf orderings of this six-chart input, B can distinguish the arms on its first loss. In **7,680** it even loses the same leaf address in both. The separate archive check covered **2,507 historical input states** and **8,046 paired withdrawals**. Only 288 pairs changed the cover size between arms. The six-chart input extends that archived domain. The counts enumerate cases, not physical probabilities. [Replay and local records](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/blob/de5056da7a5071a9634a3d5018fd3ec7e9fdc8ca/ResearchHistory/UQCF-GEM/research/physical-bridge/geometry_recovery_paths/receiver_event_order/four_leaf_local_passage/replay.json) · [Historical archive checks](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/blob/de5056da7a5071a9634a3d5018fd3ec7e9fdc8ca/ResearchHistory/UQCF-GEM/research/physical-bridge/geometry_recovery_paths/receiver_event_order/four_leaf_local_passage/verification.json).
