## What is being connected

The candidate chain is pruning, then a support update, then an action response.

1. Native P1 acts on address-indexed grades. For one pruning of source u into retained address p, the pushforward removes u as its own address and adds its grade to p: z'_p = z_p + z_u. That grade rule is inherited P1 structure inside its stated domain. It is not, by itself, a product-pair weight or a receiver report.
2. V1696.5 carries a support-incidence cochain, C_support, over source, support, and provenance nodes touched by a product pair. The recovered definition includes source incidence, support-block incidence, dependency-edge incidence, an orientation sign, and an admissibility mask. That is historical relational structure. It is richer than a scalar label. It is not yet a rule for which action weight changes when one native address is pruned.
3. V1694.1 and V1694.4 define a product action whose weights multiply squared product residuals, and they separate support variation from connection variation. Those are historical formulas with their original no-law qualification. They do not select the support change that an actual pruning realizes.

The letters p and q in the historical product-pair formulas are not the P1 owner p. P1 is not the historical connection transport. The four weights in the V1696.5 assessment also stay distinct: V1691.1 omega_ab weights a product-pair inner product; V1691.1 w_k weights algebraic components; V1696.0 alpha_a aggregates fine pair covectors; V1696.5 signature-distance weights compare blocks such as role, derivative profile, and the support cochain. None of those texts defines alpha_a, omega_ab, or a signature-distance weight as the extensive grade of a P1 address set.

## Two calculations that are both consistent

The weighting used for the comparison is conditional. It is not a law read out of V1696.5. Suppose a pair's source support has already been identified with a set S of native addresses, and suppose the pair weight is the extensive grade of that set:

w(S) = sum over v in S of z_v.

Both the identification and this equation are supplied in the assessment. Finite additivity then makes the membership matrix the only coefficient. No fitted real weights are required inside that restricted proposal. That is a consequence of the proposed meaning of weight. It is not a derivation of the meaning.

Apply the same rule before and after the pruning of u into p.

| Support before | Support after | Grade the after-support sees | Conditional change in w |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {p} | {p} | z'_p = z_p + z_u | z_u |
| {p, u} | {p} | z'_p = z_p + z_u | 0 |

The second row is grade conservation. The before-support already contains both addresses, and P1 moves z_u into p, so the sum is unchanged. In the assessment's notation the same zero appears when the before-support is the full preimage of the after-support. The first row is the retained singleton at both ends: the after-grade includes z_u and the before-weight did not.

Both rows are mathematically consistent with P1 and with the supplied rule w(S). They are contrasting conditional assignments. Dot's comment [6716989021](https://github.com/proteinfoldingengine/WetLabEngine/pull/111#issuecomment-6716989021) says they are not proved to be equivalent V1696.5 classes, and they are not proved to be admissible native alternatives. Whenever the admitted contrast changes z_u, the two rows have different event coefficients. The inherited pair-support update, together with the weighting rule, has to decide which support history is the actual one. Choosing {p} because Delta w = z_u matches a desired owner signal would insert the relation being sought. The P1 working note makes the same point with two supplied substitutions of one nonzero residual: total grade is conserved, so the action increment is 0; the selected p-coordinate gives an increment z_u. The bare action and P1 do not choose between them.

A unique pair representative is not a universal extra requirement. On the proposed grade-weight route, two representatives give the same weight on an admitted domain D exactly when their incidence-row difference annihilates every grade in D. On the full formal nonnegative cone that forces identical rows. That is a class-level well-definedness check. It does not select the native support. V1696.0's linear pair transport does not fill the gap: V1696.2 discards vector transport as the active identity target and keeps equivalence classes.

A separate unreviewed check, the incidence-update note at `70dd277e`, compares an existential direct-image of pair supports with an additive pushforward of those weights. They agree for every formal grade only when, on each ancestral fiber, the fine pairs that merge are disjoint and cover each touched fiber completely. In its displayed examples the lone surviving `{p}` row has discrepancy `(0, 1)`, and duplicate `{p}` rows give `(-1, 1)`. The second can cancel on one grade vector while the matrix identity still fails. Agreement on one preparation does not establish the rule. Action comparison also has to account for changed residual costs. Receiver ownership is a further obligation. Nothing in that algebra assigns the scalar to the P1 owner.
