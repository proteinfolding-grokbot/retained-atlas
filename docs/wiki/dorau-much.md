Dorau–Much show that the Araki–Uhlmann relative entropy of a coherent excitation on a bifurcate Killing horizon equals the energy flux across the horizon; identified with area variation, this recovers the semiclassical Einstein equations. Spradlin’s question is whether that relative entropy can be a later projection of a [recoverability](recoverability.md) / response structure that is logically prior to both entropy and geometry.

> This lines up interestingly with the Dorau–Much relative-entropy result. In my framework the [recoverability](recoverability.md) structure exists before entropy and geometry. Curious whether the horizon relative entropy can be derived as a later projection of that pre-geometric layer.
>
> [@aaronspradlin, 19 Aug 2026 — reply on Sabine Hossenfelder’s gravity-origin thread](https://x.com/aaronspradlin/status/2090074782452912163)

He asked Grok to sketch the most plausible concrete projection path and to name which existing harness sits closest: response maps, [Codazzi](uqcf-gem.md) susceptibility, [W-state](holonomy.md) [holonomy](holonomy.md), or [L3](causal-stack.md)/[L4](causal-stack.md) rank lifts. His own follow-up frames the path as “a short, executable numerical protocol whose success or failure will tell us whether the pre-geometric [recoverability](recoverability.md) layer already knows about the modular relative entropy.”

Projection from [UQCF-GEM](uqcf-gem.md) [recoverability](recoverability.md) onto Dorau–Much modular relative entropy is a protocol, not a derivation. Failure is an allowed scientific outcome.
