## 20 Jul — A\_G is a support poset, not a unique history[#](#20-jul-a-g-is-a-support-poset-not-a-unique-history)

The [admissible body A\_G](recoverability.md) names which collections are allowed. Membership is not a ranking. Provenance only forces a support-poset by inclusion — one collection can be strictly more pruned than another. A law that would pick a unique path through [A\_G](recoverability.md) is still open. That is the next theorem named that day, not a result.

> Provenance gives a canonical support-poset, but a unique pruning sequence is still open.

> [@aaronspradlin, 20 Jul 2026](https://x.com/aaronspradlin/status/2079039351451951450)

A same-day reply posed the working hypothesis for later pruning: at the relative boundary of [A\_G](recoverability.md) no full-rank global extension exists, and a universal forbidden support sector is shared by every compatible extension. Precursor of physical gravity remains open — not a theorem.
