## 29–30 Jan — first validation[#](#29-30-jan-first-validation)

29 Jan 2025 opened an astrophysical validation thread: weak-lensing κ and velocity-dispersion mass estimates without dataset-specific tuning. Same thread: a DES/HSC/CFHTLenS/KiDS/RCSLenS + SDSS DR17 + Chandra/XMM mass-scaling chart. The chart used an unfilled Python placeholder for fit coefficients; this atlas does not invent them. 30 Jan posted the ICL-growth equation.

MICL​(z)=0.12(1+z)−1.48e−0.65z

Not claimed

The 30 Jan 2025 ICL-growth equation and the 29 Jan mass-scaling chart are archived so the first-validation week is readable. They are not 2026 supported cluster-mass or ICL results.
